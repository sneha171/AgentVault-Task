Kickstarting AgentVault's First 50 Users
1. Target User Segments
Segment 1: Active Crypto Traders

Why them?
They trade regularly and are always looking for tools that help them make faster and better decisions.

Pain Point:
Too much market information, difficult to track opportunities quickly.

Where to find them?
Twitter (X), Discord crypto communities, Telegram groups, Reddit.

Segment 2: Student Investors / Beginner Traders

Why them?
They are interested in crypto but need guidance and easy-to-use tools.

Pain Point:
Lack of experience and confusion about market trends.

Where to find them?
College blockchain clubs, LinkedIn, Discord communities, Web3 events.

2. Outreach Example

Twitter / Discord DM

Hi,

I noticed you're active in the crypto space.

We're building AgentVault, an AI-powered crypto trading assistant that helps users track opportunities and make informed decisions faster.

We're currently inviting a small group of early users to test the product and share feedback.

Would you be interested in early access?

Thanks!

3. GTM Ideas / Campaigns
1. Campus Blockchain Outreach

Connect with college blockchain clubs and offer early beta access.

2. Crypto Community Partnerships

Partner with Discord and Telegram crypto communities and host AMA sessions.

3. Referral Program

Give rewards or exclusive access to users who bring friends to the platform.

Conclusion

My focus would be on active crypto traders and student investors because they are easier to reach, actively looking for crypto tools, and can provide valuable early feedback to help AgentVault grow its first 50 users.
